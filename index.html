How it works

From a WhatsApp message to a delivered order

01

Customer messages you

ShopBot replies instantly in their language — English, Urdu, German and more — with products, deals, or order status.

→
02

It shows up on the Live Orders board in real time — item, variant, address, and total, ready for you to confirm.

→
03

Move it through Confirmed → Packed → Shipped, and send the rider the full order details on WhatsApp with one tap.
